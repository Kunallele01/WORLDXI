Researched 2026-09-12 after the user asked to cover roughly 2005-2025. Nothing built yet; this is the map. Currently loaded: 2014/15-2023/24 ([[dream-xi-seasons-2015-2019]]).

**Every input exists back to 2005/06 — the blocker was never the data:**
- Fixtures/tables: worldfootballR mirror `match_results/{ENG,ESP}` — decades deep, no browser.
- Player stats 2009/10-2022/23: ALREADY ON DISK in `etl/wfr/big5_player_{standard,shooting,keepers,misc}.rds` (minutes, goals, non-pen goals, cards, shots, SoT, save %), full PL + La Liga coverage.
- Player stats 2005/06-2008/09: fbref.com in the logged-in browser only (Cloudflare blocks scripts). Verified 2005-06: PL 553 / La Liga 540 player rows with minutes, goals_pens, cards, birth_year, shots, shots_on_target; keeper save %; stable `/en/players/<id>/` links. Same-origin fetch() from an FBref tab pulls the other stat pages without navigating.
- FIFA ratings: our Kaggle file (`etl/archive/male_players.csv`) is FIFA 15-24 ONLY. sofifa.com has FIFA 07-14 and loads fine in the logged-in browser (curl gets 403). With our edition N+1 -> season N rule, FIFA 07-14 cover 2005/06-2012/13; FIFA 15 already covers 2013/14. League-filtered roster pages are open and paginate (lg 13 = PL, lg 53 = La Liga, 60 per page, `offset=`), roughly 160 page loads for all 8 editions x 2 leagues. **Player detail pages need a sofifa sign-in** — so EA position grids for old editions are unverified. The user would have to create/log in; I can't.
- Transfermarkt values in the mirror only go back to 2010 — not usable as a 2005 anchor.

**npxG proxy — measured, passes:** npxG does not exist before 2014/15 anywhere public. Proxy fitted on 2014/15-2018/19 only: `npxG/90 = -0.0072 + 0.0390*shots/90 + 0.0691*SoT/90 + 0.4152*goals/90` (player R² 0.85). Scored blind on 159 club-seasons from 2019/20-2023/24 through the full attack + log-defence + Poisson pipeline: goals-for r 0.904 vs 0.863 with real npxG; points r 0.903 vs 0.898, RMSE 7.6 vs 8.1. Not worse. Caveat: part of the edge is that the proxy contains real goals, so it is partly fitting the outcome. Fine for the sim, but don't claim it is a better measure of chance quality.

**Stats-only ratings (no FIFA) — fallback only:** predicting FIFA overall from 2005-available stats + club points + age, held-out seasons: RMSE 2.78, 84% within 3 points, BUT systematically underrates 85+ players by 1.3-3.1 points (regression to the mean). Defenders are weakest (R² 0.25). Stars are the point of a draft, so use real sofifa ratings where possible.

**One Two (joinonetwo.com Dream Team — the app that inspired ERA XI):** PL only, 2000/01-2025/26. Their own words: "Our ratings combine raw season stats with general football reputation. These are unofficial ratings." Six categories (Fin/Cre/Carry/Build/Def/Phys; GK Ref/Pos/Phys/Dist) plus trait tags and an LLM-style blurb per player. Their 2000/01 cards quote pass % and clearances per 90 (no free source has these) and credit Rio Ferdinand with 35 Leeds starts despite his November 2000 arrival, so treat their old-season numbers as reputation-generated, not data. Do not lift their dataset: it's their work, and it has errors.

**Why:** the user wants 2005-2025 and was stuck believing the data didn't exist.

User approved: (1) position-template grids for gridless players, (2) calibrated stats->FIFA rating for the 26 unanchored regulars, (3) the load. Both implemented in `stage_seasons_2010_2014.py`.
- LESSON that cost a re-stage: the scraped editions hold only two leagues, so short Spanish names were "unique" but wrong — 15 bindings (José Ángel the Sporting LB -> "Nene" a Granada ST, several "Roberto"s -> namesake keepers). Fixed with `FifaLookup.get_profile(position_hint=...)`: reject a candidate whose EA positions contradict FBref's (GK vs outfield; DF-only vs FW-only) and KEEP SEARCHING. The new seasons pass hints through `PositionHintedLookup` (both get and get_profile, so rating and grid can't come from different men); attributes.py and the 2014-24 pipeline deliberately untouched. The verifier's floor check (no regular under 55) is what caught it — a plausible wrong rating would have passed. The modern seasons have NOT been re-audited with this guard.
- Route that worked for browser data: a user-approved in-page download. Localhost POST hangs (Chrome local-network prompt), clipboard needs focus, page-text caps at 50KB inline.

- ERA GAP FOUND AFTER LOADING (decision pending with user, 2026-09-12): 2009/10-2013/14 squad_strength runs ~2-3 points below 2015/16+ (PL ~77.5 vs ~79.3; La Liga ~76.8 vs ~78.9), so 97 of the 200 new club-seasons sit in draft quartile 1. NOT the scrape (same-player prime-age drift across the sofifa->Kaggle switch, FIFA 14->15, is -0.05) and NOT the form modifier (final-minus-FIFA-base is ~0 in both eras). It is EA INFLATING RATINGS in two steps, FIFA 15->16 (+1.57) and 16->17 (+1.34), flat otherwise. Inflation is band-dependent: excess over the normal year-to-year drift is ~+1.4/step under 70, +1.2 at 70-74, +1.05 at 75-79, +0.85 at 80-84, +0.9 at 85+. The already-loaded 2014/15 (FIFA 16 anchor) is also ~1.3 low — the 2014-19 expansion's "no era handicap needed" conclusion averaged it away. RESOLVED 2026-09-12 — user chose option 2 (rescale; options 1 = leave EA numbers, 3 = per-era draft tiers only were rejected). `etl/rating_scale.py` `to_modern_scale(edition, rating)`: per-band excess measured against THAT BAND's own normal drift, interpolated between band centres; ≤FIFA 15 ≈ +2.8 (sub-70) → +1.8 (80+), FIFA 16 ≈ +1.0..+1.4, 17+ unchanged. After: leftover inflation within ±0.3 in every band. A FIRST VERSION with straight-line fits was WRONG at the top (left +1.1 in the 85+ band; Messi 94 -> 94.3) because normal drift isn't linear in rating — its own before/after check caught it. Applied as targeted updates, not a reload: `apply_rating_scale_db.py` (6,378 rows: 2009-14 take re-staged values, 2014/15 and FIFA-16-anchored 2015/16 rows get current + correction), then squad_strength backfill. Outcome: season mean squad strength flat (PL 79.2-80.2, La Liga 78.5-79.3 across all 15 seasons); 2009-14 tiers 45/53/51/51; Barcelona 2010/11 = 87.2, strongest club-season in the DB (Messi 96, Xavi 93, Iniesta 92). PL 2009/10-2010/11 sit slightly high (79.8-80.2) — within noise, not chased. Defence fit re-run over all 600 club-seasons: slope -0.0741 vs app's -0.0729, magic XI 20.9/20.1 conceded vs 20.5/19.7 — app constants deliberately NOT changed.
